# IPRX Dataset License (Draft)

**Status: DRAFT v1, pending legal review.** This draft is not a binding contract until counsel has reviewed it and Verae publishes an adopted version.

**Licensor:** Verae  
**Governing law:** the laws of the State of New Hampshire. Venue is in the courts of the State of New Hampshire.  
**Billing:** annual (per year).

A valid Verae account is required for every organization, whether free or paid, and for every private individual who uses the dataset or requests an API token. A valid account means a verified email address, a required organization-size category, and acceptance of this license. Accounts are created with billing disabled. No payment card is required to open an account or to start a trial.

## 1. Submissions become the property of Verae

All nominations, removal requests, evidence, supporting files, and other submissions made to IPRX become the property of Verae. Verae may use, retain, publish (in redacted form where this license or the public site requires it), timestamp, and otherwise process those submissions in operating IPRX.

## 2. No warranty as to the validity of removal requests

Verae makes no warranty itself of the validity of removal requests, or of any nomination, listing, or delisting decision. Listings and removals are provided as-is. A passing technical check (including DKIM) is not a warranty that the request is meritorious.

## 3. How the removal procedure protects the public

A listed address is not removed solely because someone asked. A removal request is recorded and stamped, then must prove control of a domain tied to the IP (the PTR hostname’s registrable domain, or the RDAP/WHOIS organization or abuse-contact domain) by a DKIM-signed message that contains the request token. A failed check is recorded and stamped but the request does not advance. A passing request still requires an administrator’s approval before the address is delisted. The add and the remove are both written to the database and stamped on Verae, so the public history of why an address was listed or removed remains provable.

## 4. Annual prices for use of the dataset

Prices are billed **per year**. Each company or organizational branch is assessed on its own annual revenue, not on the revenue of a parent or of sibling branches.

**Free tier (unchanged):**

- Certified 501(c)(3) non-profits, with documentation: $0 per year
- Schools, with documentation: $0 per year
- Private individuals: $0 per year

Non-profits and schools must upload or attach documentation (for example an IRS determination letter, or school accreditation). Their account status stays `pending verification` until an administrator approves the documentation.

**Paid tiers — companies or organizational branches, each assessed on its own annual revenue:**

- Companies or branches with under $500,000 annual revenue: $250 per year
- Companies or branches with $500,000 to $5,000,000 annual revenue: $2,500 per year
- Companies or branches with $5,000,000 to $50,000,000 annual revenue: $25,000 per year
- Companies or branches with $50,000,000 to $250,000,000 annual revenue: $250,000 per year
- Companies or branches with $250,000,000 to $1,000,000,000 annual revenue: $2,500,000 per year
- Companies or branches with $1,000,000,000 or more in annual revenue: $25,000,000 per year

Separate companies with multiple divisions have 30 days to enter into an agreement with the Verae.com sales department.

On account setup the requestor must choose an organization-size category from: 501(c)(3) non-profit; school; individual; or one of the company-or-branch revenue tiers above. The category is stored on the account and stamped on Verae as a hashed record. Changes to the category are also stamped.

## 5. Fourteen-day free trial (paid tiers)

Any paid-tier organization (companies or branches) receives 14 days of full dataset use at no charge, starting when its Verae account is verified and set up. A valid account means a verified email plus acceptance of this license, with the required organization-size category.

- There is one trial per organization or branch.
- No payment card is required. The account stays billing-disabled during the trial.
- Before day 14 ends, the organization must sign up for its annual tier, or, for multi-division companies, enter an agreement with Verae.com sales within the existing 30-day window, or stop using the dataset.
- Expiry of a trial does not block nominations or removal requests. The trial concerns dataset use only.

The start of a trial is stamped on Verae as a hashed event. The account page shows the days remaining.

## 6. Accounts, tokens, and dataset access

Every organization and every individual must have a Verae account. Dataset access and API tokens require one. Token issue, email verification, license acceptance, organization-size selection, trial start, and later category changes are stamped on Verae as hashed records. Email addresses and tokens are never written to the chain in plaintext. Public pages redact contact details.

## 7. Acceptance

Using the IPRX dataset, requesting an API token, or submitting a nomination or removal after this draft is adopted constitutes acceptance of the then-current license version. Acceptance is recorded and stamped with the license version and the document hash.
