# IPRX dataset pricing

**Status: DRAFT v1, pending legal review.** Prices are billed **per year**. The governing law is the State of New Hampshire; venue is in New Hampshire courts. See the [dataset license](license.md) for the full draft terms.

Every organization, and every private individual, must have a Verae account to use the dataset or to request an API token. A valid account is a verified email, a required organization-size category, and acceptance of the license. Accounts are opened with billing disabled. No payment card is required to open an account or to start a trial. Each company or organizational branch is assessed on its own annual revenue.

## Free tier

| Category | Annual price |
| --- | ---: |
| Certified 501(c)(3) non-profit (documentation required; pending verification until an administrator approves it) | $0 |
| School (documentation required; pending verification until an administrator approves it) | $0 |
| Private individual | $0 |

## Paid tiers (companies or branches)

| Category | Annual price |
| --- | ---: |
| Companies or branches with under $500,000 annual revenue | $250 |
| Companies or branches with $500,000 to $5,000,000 annual revenue | $2,500 |
| Companies or branches with $5,000,000 to $50,000,000 annual revenue | $25,000 |
| Companies or branches with $50,000,000 to $250,000,000 annual revenue | $250,000 |
| Companies or branches with $250,000,000 to $1,000,000,000 annual revenue | $2,500,000 |
| Companies or branches with $1,000,000,000 or more in annual revenue | $25,000,000 |

Separate companies with multiple divisions have 30 days to enter into an agreement with the Verae.com sales department.

## 14-day free trial

Paid-tier companies or branches receive 14 days of full dataset use at no charge once a Verae account is verified and set up (verified email, organization-size category, and accepted license). One trial per organization or branch. No card is required; the account stays billing-disabled during the trial. Before day 14 ends, the organization must take its annual tier, or a multi-division company must enter a sales agreement within the 30-day window, or it must stop using the dataset. Trial expiry does not block nominations or removal requests.

On setup you choose an organization-size category from the free and paid lists above. That category is stored on the account and stamped on Verae as a hashed record, as are later changes.
